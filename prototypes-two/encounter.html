Your safety comes before arguing about rights

Do not resist. Stay calm, keep hands visible, and comply only as needed for safety. You can challenge conduct later.

Quick safety checklist

✓ Stay calm and polite. Keep hands visible where the officer can see them.
✓ Ask for the reason for the stop and the officer's identity/agency.
✓ Only show documents requested. You can note details (badge, plate, time).
✓ You do not have to consent to a search. But stay safe — don't physically resist.
✓ Document when safe: note time, location, agency, badge number, vehicle plate.
