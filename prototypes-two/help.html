📝 Official Complaint Channels

Police Complaints Response Unit (CRU)

Official Nigeria Police Force channel for complaints against police officers — bribery, misconduct, harassment, unlawful arrest, extrajudicial actions.

🌐 npf.gov.ng/complaint 📞 0805 700 0001 / 0805 700 0002 📧 cru@npf.gov.ng 📱 WhatsApp: 0805 700 0001 Verified Jan 2025

Police Public Complaint Rapid Response Unit (PCRRU)

Alternative police complaint channel for urgent response. Handles same categories as CRU.

📞 0805 700 0003 / 0805 700 0004 📧 pcrru@npf.gov.ng 🌐 npf.gov.ng Verified Jan 2025

FRSC Complaints & Feedback

For complaints against Federal Road Safety Corps officers — misconduct, bribery, improper stops, ticket disputes.

🌐 frsc.gov.ng/complaints 📞 0800 377 222 (toll-free) 📧 info@frsc.gov.ng 📱 WhatsApp: 0807 769 0000 Verified Jan 2025

National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)

Independent body for human rights violations by any security agency — unlawful arrest, torture, extrajudicial killing, discrimination.

🌐 nhrc.gov.ng/complaints 📞 0800 647 2478 (toll-free) 📧 complaints@nhrc.gov.ng 📍 Headquarters: 19 Aguiyi Ironsi St, Maitama, Abuja Verified Jan 2025

Investigates administrative injustice by government agencies, including police and paramilitary.

🌐 publiccomplaintscommission.gov.ng 📞 0800 225 577 (toll-free) 📧 info@publiccomplaintscommission.gov.ng Verified Jan 2025

For complaints against Lagos State Traffic Management Authority officers.

🌐 lastma.lagosstate.gov.ng 📞 0800 225 577 (toll-free) 📧 lastma@lagosstate.gov.ng Verified Jan 2025

📋 After an Encounter — What to Do

  1. Get to safety first. Your physical safety is the priority.
  2. Document everything. Time, location, officer details, vehicle plate, what happened, witnesses.
  3. Get medical attention if injured — request a medical report.
  4. File a formal complaint with the appropriate body above within 24–48 hours if possible.
  5. Keep copies of all complaints, receipts, medical reports, and correspondence.
  6. Seek legal advice from Legal Aid Council or an NGO if your rights were violated.
  7. Follow up. Get a complaint reference number and follow up in writing.
