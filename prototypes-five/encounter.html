Stay calm. You have rights.

Six short steps for the moment you're stopped. Read them now — or save this page for offline use before your next trip.

✓ Reviewed · Oct 2026 ⤓ Save for offline ⎙ Print 🔗 Share

The six steps

01 Pull over safely 02 Hands visible 03 Greet, then ask 04 Documents only 05 Note or record 06 The two questions
Call 112 now
1
First 10 seconds

Pull over safely

Indicate, stop fully off the lane, switch off the engine. Sudden moves raise tension — yours and theirs.

💡 Night stops: interior light on. It signals nothing to hide and helps everyone see clearly.
2
While stopping

Hands visible, window down

Keep hands on the wheel. Move slowly and announce what you're reaching for before you reach.

💡 "I'm reaching for my licence in the wallet now" — one sentence prevents most misunderstandings.
3
Opening line

Greet, then ask one calm question

"Good afternoon, officer. Please, what is the issue?" Politeness costs nothing and de-escalates almost everything.

4
Documents

Provide required documents only

Driver's licence and vehicle papers where required. Your phone, bag and privacy stay with you unless the law says otherwise.

5
Protect the record

You may note or record, where safe

Note the officer's name, unit and vehicle number. Recording rules vary — check the phone-search scenario for the details.

Can I record the encounter? →
6
If it goes further

If detained or unsure, ask the two questions

"Am I under arrest?" and "On what grounds?" If the answer is no, you may ask whether you are free to go — calmly, once.

💡 Then stop talking. Anything after this should happen with a lawyer informed — the help directory has free options.

Words that help, words that hurt

Say this

  • 🟢"Good afternoon, officer. Please, what is the issue?"
  • 🟢"Here is my licence. Is there anything else you need?"
  • 🟢"Am I under arrest? On what grounds?"
  • 🟢"I'd like to note your name and unit number, please."

Avoid saying

  • 🔴"Do you know who I am?" — escalates fast.
  • 🔴"How much will you take?" — that's a bribe, and a crime.
  • 🔴Arguing case law at the roadside — note it, contest it later.
  • 🔴Sudden movements toward the glove box unannounced.
