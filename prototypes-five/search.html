Arrest · Nigeria Police (NPF)

Must I unlock my phone if detained?

No — and you cannot be forced to give up passcodes or biometrics without proper legal process. If your phone is seized, insist on an inventory receipt and record the officer's details.

Impoundment · Lagos VIS / DVS

Can they seize my phone along with the vehicle?

No. Impoundment covers the vehicle, not your personal belongings. You are entitled to remove personal items — including your phone — before the vehicle is taken, and to a receipt.
