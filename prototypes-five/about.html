About this guide

What this is, what it isn't, how every answer gets reviewed, and why we collect almost nothing about you.

Scope & honesty

A guide you can trust starts with being clear about what it can and cannot do.

What this is

  • ✓Plain-language summaries of Nigerian law on roadside encounters, in one place.
  • ✓Source-backed: every answer links the actual Act, regulation or official guidance.
  • ✓Safety-first: practical steps ordered by what protects you in the moment.
  • ✓Free and anonymous — no account, no data trail, works offline.

What this is not

  • ✗Not legal advice. It's reviewed information about the law, not advice on your specific case.
  • ✗Not a lawyer. For your situation, the help directory connects you to real counsel.
  • ✗Not a guarantee of outcomes. Enforcement practice varies; the law is one part of the encounter.
  • ✗Not complete. Coverage grows as legal review finishes — gaps are labelled, not hidden.

How every scenario gets reviewed

Nothing publishes without Nigerian legal review. Dates and reviewers are shown on every page.

Drafted from sources

Researchers read the Act, regulation or official guidance and draft the scenario in plain language.

Lawyer review

A Nigerian lawyer checks every claim, the exceptions, and the practical options against the sources.

Published & dated

Approved scenarios go live with reviewer, last-reviewed and next-review dates shown publicly.

Corrected openly

Flagged issues trigger a review; material changes are tracked in a public change history.

Privacy

People search this guide for sensitive reasons. The design reflects that.

Your search history is
nobody's business.

No accounts, no tracking pixels on content, and no selling of data — ever. Not in v1, not with ads, not "anonymised".

Feedback is anonymous by design: it stores what you flagged, never who you are.

Corrections & feedback

Every scenario page has a one-tap feedback row — helpful, confusing, outdated, or missing.

Flagged items go straight into the review queue, anonymously. Material corrections are published with a visible change note, and the review trail on each page shows the history.

Legal disclaimer

KnowYourRights NG is an information resource, not a law firm. Content is reviewed by Nigerian legal practitioners before publication, but it does not constitute legal advice and no lawyer–client relationship is created by using it. Laws change and enforcement practice varies by state and agency; scenarios carry their last review date and should be read with it. For advice on a specific matter, consult a qualified legal practitioner — the help directory lists verified free options. Encounters with enforcement agencies carry real risks; the safety-first ordering of guidance reflects this, and following it cannot guarantee any particular outcome.
