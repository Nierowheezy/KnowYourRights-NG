Police have stopped my vehicle. What should I do?

Last reviewed: 12 Oct 2026 • Status: Lawyer-reviewed

First: Stay calm, prioritize safety. Do not physically resist. The legal position depends on the reason for the stop and who is stopping you.

Short answer

Pauses allowed only for lawful reasons. You must provide identity and vehicle documents when lawfully requested. You may ask the reason for the stop politely.

What the law says

Nigeria Police Act 2020 and FRSC Act allow stops for traffic offences, suspicion, or operational reasons. Officers must state a lawful basis. Random stops without grounds may be unlawful, but safety takes priority in the moment.

What you can do now

  • Keep hands visible, stay in vehicle unless told otherwise
  • Ask politely: “May I know the reason for the stop?”
  • Provide requested documents calmly
  • Note badge number and vehicle details when safe

What to avoid

Don't argue, run, or physically resist. Don't offer money. Don't hand over your phone unless required by law and you understand the consequences.
