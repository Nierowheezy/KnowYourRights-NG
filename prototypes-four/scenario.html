Scenario Guidance

Step-by-step guidance for this situation

Remember

Stay calm, keep hands visible, be polite but firm. Your safety is the priority. This is general information, not legal advice.

What to do

  1. Stay calm and assess

    Pull over safely if driving. Turn off engine, keep hands visible on steering wheel.

  2. Ask why you're being stopped

    Politely ask the officer the reason for the stop. Stay respectful but don't volunteer extra information.

  3. Provide required documents

    Only provide what's legally required. Ask to see their ID if unsure. Never hand over original documents unnecessarily.

  4. Document the encounter

    Note badge numbers, time, location, and names if possible. If safe, note details on your phone.

What NOT to do

  • Don't argue or become confrontational
  • Don't consent to searches you don't have to
  • Don't hand over extra documents or items
  • Don't sign anything without understanding it

Your rights

  • You have the right to remain silent
  • You can ask for the officer's identity
  • You don't have to consent to a search
  • You can document the encounter if safe to do so
