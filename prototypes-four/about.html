About KnowYourRights NG

Empowering Nigerians with knowledge of their rights during roadside encounters

Our mission

KnowYourRights NG provides free, accurate, and easy-to-understand information about your rights during roadside encounters with law enforcement and other authorities in Nigeria. We believe everyone deserves to know their rights and how to navigate these situations safely.

What we do

  • Provide clear, step-by-step guidance for common roadside scenarios
  • Explain your rights in plain language, not legal jargon
  • Offer safety-first advice that prioritizes your wellbeing
  • Connect you with emergency resources and legal assistance
  • Keep information up-to-date with current Nigerian procedures

Important disclaimer

KnowYourRights NG provides general legal information, not legal advice or representation. The information on this site is for educational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional. Laws and procedures can change, and each situation is unique.

Source-backed

Our guidance is based on Nigerian laws, constitution, and established procedures. We strive to provide accurate information, but we recommend consulting a qualified legal practitioner for specific legal advice.
