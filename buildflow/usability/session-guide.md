# Usability Session Guide

## Before the session

- Confirm the participant understands the purpose.
- Explain that the app is a guide, not legal advice.
- Ask for verbal consent to take anonymised notes.
- Confirm no recording unless separately agreed.

## Script

**Welcome**

Thank you for helping. We are testing the guide, not you. There are no wrong answers.

**Think-aloud**

As you use the app, say what you are looking at, what you expect, and what feels confusing.

**Tasks**

1. Imagine you are stopped by the police in your car. Find guidance for that situation.
2. Imagine an officer asks you to unlock your phone. Find what the guide says.
3. Find where to report police misconduct.
4. Pick one answer and show me the source behind it.
5. Find what the app does when you are offline and tell me what the release date means.

**After each task**

- Was the answer easy or hard to find? Rate 1 to 5.
- Did you trust the answer? Rate 1 to 5.
- What would you change?

**Close**

- Anything else that felt unclear or unsafe?
- Thank the participant.

## Ethics

- Do not collect names, contact details, or device identifiers.
- Do not ask about real encounters or personal legal matters.
- Stop the session if the participant becomes uncomfortable.
