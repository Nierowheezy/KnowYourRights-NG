# Usability Test Plan

## Purpose

Validate that everyday road users can find answer, understand it, navigate the app, and trust the safety-first guidance.

## Participants

Recruit 5 to 8 participants:

- 2 drivers or riders with regular road travel
- 1 commercial driver or dispatch rider
- 1 public transport user or pedestrian
- 1 civil society advocate
- Optional: 1 lawyer or legal reviewer for comprehension feedback

No personal data is stored. Sessions are moderated and notes are anonymised.

## Tasks

1. Find guidance for a police stop and search of a car.
2. Find what to do if an officer asks for a phone unlock.
3. Find the official complaint channel for a police misconduct issue.
4. Locate the source behind one answer.
5. Find the offline explainer and say what the release date means.

## Success criteria

| Task | Success | Time target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opens the correct scenario | under 60 seconds |
| 2 | Opens the correct scenario or searches correctly | under 90 seconds |
| 3 | Reaches Help directory and opens a verified channel | under 90 seconds |
| 4 | Opens a source link or source card | under 60 seconds |
| 5 | Explains source-check vs release date | under 120 seconds |

## Metrics

- Task completion rate
- Time on task
- Number of wrong turns
- Comprehension rating (1–5)
- Trust rating (1–5)
- Qualitative notes per task

## Outputs

- `session-guide.md` used for each session
- `findings.md` completed after the first round
- Prioritised fixes mapped to build-plan items
