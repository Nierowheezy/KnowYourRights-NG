About KnowYourRights NG

Free, source-backed legal guidance for every Nigerian

We make Nigerian law accessible during roadside encounters. No legal jargon. No account required. Just your rights.

Our approach

Three principles guide everything we publish.

📚

Source-backed

Every scenario cites the exact Act, Regulation, Court decision, or official guidance. You can verify every claim yourself.

☛

Lawyer-reviewed

Nigerian legal practitioners review every scenario before publication. Review dates and reviewer names are shown.

💻

Safety-first

Guidance prioritises your physical safety in the moment. Legal technicalities come second to getting home alive.

Scope & limitations

What we cover — and what we don't.

We cover

  • Police roadside stops and searches
  • FRSC checkpoints and particulars
  • Customs vehicle stops and seizures
  • VIO inspections and impoundment
  • Arrest procedures and station rights
  • Bribe demands and reporting
  • Complaint channels and legal aid

We don't cover

  • Court proceedings or litigation strategy
  • Contract or property law disputes
  • Family, employment, or commercial law
  • State-specific bylaws (beyond major agencies)
  • Legal advice for your specific case
  • Representation or lawyer referrals

Review process

How we ensure accuracy and keep content current.

1. Draft

Research & drafting

Legal researchers compile primary sources — Acts, Regulations, Court decisions, official guidance — and draft plain-language scenarios.

2. Review

Legal practitioner review

A Nigerian lawyer verifies legal accuracy, checks for updates, and approves or requests revisions. Reviewer name and date recorded.

3. Publish

Publication & versioning

Approved scenarios go live with review metadata. Changes trigger re-review. All versions archived for transparency.

4. Monitor

Ongoing monitoring

Scheduled re-reviews, legislative change alerts, and user feedback flags trigger updates. Outdated content is marked.
