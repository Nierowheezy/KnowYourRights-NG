Priority: Stay safe first

Your safety comes before arguing about rights

Do not resist. Stay calm, keep hands visible, and comply only as needed for safety. You can challenge conduct later.

Quick safety checklist

✓ Stay calm and polite. Keep hands visible.
✓ Ask for reason for stop and officer's identity/agency.
✓ Only show documents requested. You can note details.
✓ Do not consent to search if unsure. Stay safe.
✓ Document when safe. Note time, location, agency, badge.
