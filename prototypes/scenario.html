Police have stopped my vehicle

What should I do if the police pull me over?

Short answer

Stay calm and prioritize safety. Ask for the reason for the stop and officer's identity/agency. Only show documents requested. You can note details and challenge conduct later if it exceeds lawful limits.

What the law says

Police may stop vehicles for lawful purposes (e.g., traffic offences or reasonable suspicion). You may be asked to produce valid documents. Powers depend on the circumstance and applicable law.

Always check the specific legal basis for any request made.

What you can do now

• Stay calm, keep hands visible, pull over safely
• Ask: "Officer, may I know the reason for this stop?"
• Ask for name/badge number and agency
• Provide documents only if requested and it is safe
• Note time, location, plate, officer details

What to avoid

• Physical resistance or arguing aggressively
• Handing over originals unnecessarily
• Consenting to search without understanding
• Recording in a way that escalates danger

Important exceptions

Powers may differ by agency and context. Some stops relate to specific offences; state procedures may vary. When in doubt, prioritize safety.
