About this guide

Scope, limitations, and how content is reviewed

What this is

KnowYourRights NG is a free, mobile-first, searchable guide to Nigerian rights and practical options during roadside and related enforcement encounters. It organizes information by situation and links to authoritative sources.

Important limitations

• This provides general legal information, not legal advice or representation
• It cannot guarantee outcomes or prevent arrest/fines/seizure
• Laws change and procedures vary by state; exceptions may apply
• It cannot intervene in an active incident
• No personal data is collected for core use

Legal review

Substantive scenarios are reviewed by a Nigerian lawyer before publication. Each scenario shows review status and last-reviewed date. Content is updated when laws or procedures change.

Corrections

If you spot an error or outdated information, please report it. We review submissions before changing legal content.

KnowYourRights NG provides general legal information, not legal advice or representation.
